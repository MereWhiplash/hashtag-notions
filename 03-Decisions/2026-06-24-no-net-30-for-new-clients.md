# No Net-30 Terms for New Clients

**Decided:** 2026-06-24

## What was decided

New clients pay 50% deposit up front and the balance on delivery. Net-30
invoicing is available only to clients with two or more completed projects.

## Why

Two new clients in spring 2026 paid 45+ days late, which forced us to delay
a supplier order. Deposit-based billing removes the cash-flow risk, and
established clients have earned the flexibility.

## Supersedes

Nothing — first formal payment-terms decision.
