# Switch Harborview Flooring to Cascadia Hardwoods

**Decided:** 2026-07-08 (in [2026-07-08-studio-sync](../04-Meetings/2026-07-08-studio-sync.md))

## What was decided

Switch the Harborview Loft flooring order from Meridian Floor Co. to
Cascadia Hardwoods, same spec (7" white oak, character grade).

## Why

Meridian quoted a 9-week lead time — past our install window. Cascadia
holds the same board in stock (2-week delivery) at ~4% higher cost, which
fits inside the project contingency. [Marco Esposito](../02-People/Marco%20Esposito.md) has installed
Cascadia stock before and vouches for it. See [fabric-lead-times](../06-Knowledge/fabric-lead-times.md) for the
broader lead-time pattern behind this call.

## Supersedes

The original Meridian selection in the May design package (not a logged
decision — predates this vault).
