Registered trade accounts get us 20–40% off retail at our main suppliers, and every project should be quoted from trade price, never retail.

Current registrations: Cascadia Hardwoods (30%), Meridian Floor Co. (25%),
Lumen & Co. lighting (40%), Fable Textiles (20% plus free swatches).

Rules of thumb:

- Always quote clients from trade price plus our standard 25% markup —
  quoting from retail then discounting confuses invoices.
- Trade status usually requires an annual minimum spend; June tracks
  whether each account is on pace (her review is open as of July 2026).
- New supplier? Register the trade account *before* the first order —
  most won't apply discounts retroactively.
