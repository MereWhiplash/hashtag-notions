Instagram followers convert poorly compared to referrals, and the account may be better treated as a portfolio for referred prospects than as a lead source.

Hunch, not yet backed by real numbers:

- Of ~12 Instagram inquiries in H1 2026, one became a paying project;
  referrals converted at roughly 4 of 6 in the same period.
- Referred prospects consistently mention having checked the Instagram
  before making contact — so the account may earn trust rather than leads.
- If true, the website relaunch should optimise for referral validation
  (portfolio depth, testimonials) over inquiry capture.

Needs a proper count of inquiry sources before acting on it — revisit once
the [[website-relaunch]] ships and we can track where forms come from.
